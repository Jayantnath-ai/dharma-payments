Why ranking by stake loses

A review is only worth its cost when the default would be risky. Large unrecoverable payments are already held when nobody looks at them, so reviewing them protects nothing. Small recoverable payments are released by default, so those are the reviews that actually guard money. Ranking by size sends the analyst to the safe pile and lets the risky pile expire.

All the protective value sits in one row. Emergency medicine encodes the same idea: the most severely injured patient is not always the one who benefits most from the next available surgeon.

Measured across 15 random seeds: ranking by what a review changes released $0 of unreviewed value at every capacity level tested, against a mean of $29,845 for arrival order. Ranking by stake performed worse than arrival order, which is what exposed the error. The full engine also cuts required analyst time from 18.9 to 5.1 hours per week (73.0% reduction, sd 0.5), almost all of it from showing reviewers evidence the system already had.
