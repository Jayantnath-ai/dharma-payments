Deferral is a bidder, not a safe harbor: escalation pays analyst labor ($60), delay cost, and a 10% chance the reviewer misses the fraud. Confidence is shrunk toward the 95% base rate as irreversibility rises, the less undoable the wire, the less the agent's forecast is trusted.

Recovery odds R come from a backward Monte Carlo over recall paths, calibrated to FBI IC3 Financial Fraud Kill Chain freeze statistics (2021–25) and the FinCEN $50k/72h international recovery rule, detection lag, recall windows, mule drain, bank cooperation, never from forecasting outcomes. Benchmarked against a confidence-threshold guardrail on 500 invoices with 5% BEC fraud: roughly half the catastrophic loss at matched friction. Prototype logic mirrors gate.py in the dharmaagi repo.
